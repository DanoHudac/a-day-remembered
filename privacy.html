A Day Remembered

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: A Day Remembered does not collect, send or share your personal data. Your moments stay on your device. The app has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

1. Who is responsible

A Day Remembered ("the app") is made and provided by Daniel Hudač, Slovak Republic ("I", "me"). You can reach me at tritonframe@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in its own private storage:

  • the moments you keep: their text, photo, colour and the time they were kept;
  • your settings, such as your reminder choices;
  • a note that the garden has been opened, if you bought it.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. I have no access to it. The app has no server and you do not create an account.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not collect personal data.
  • It does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not show ads.
  • It does not track you, in the app or across other apps and websites.
  • It does not share or sell data to anyone.

4. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo for a moment.
  • Photos: only when you choose a photo from your library for a moment. The app keeps its own copy of the photo you pick.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are created on your device; nothing is sent from a server.

Each of these is optional, and you can change them at any time in your device settings.

5. The garden purchase

If you buy the garden, the purchase is made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. The store handles the payment under its own privacy policy. The app learns from the store only whether the garden has been bought, so it can open it; it never sees your payment details, and it does not pass the purchase information on to me or anyone else.

6. Sharing a moment

A moment leaves your device only when you choose to share it. It is then handed to the app you pick in your device's share sheet, and that app's own privacy policy applies to what happens next.

7. Device backups

The app itself uploads nothing. Your device may include the app's data in its own backup (for example iCloud backup or Google's device backup), depending on your device and your settings. Those backups are controlled by you and provided by Apple or Google under their privacy policies.

8. Deleting your data

You can remove any moment in the app at any time. Removing the app from your device deletes everything it stored there. Because I hold none of your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

9. Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

10. If you contact me

If you write to me by email, I receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. I use it only to reply to you and I do not pass it on.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy in the app: tritonframe@gmail.com.
